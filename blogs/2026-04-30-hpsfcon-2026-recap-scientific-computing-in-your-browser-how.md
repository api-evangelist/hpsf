What if you didn’t need a supercomputer — or even a local Python installation — to run serious scientific computations? That’s the vision Gunj Joshi of stdlib brought to HPSFCon 2026. In “Bringing Complex Computations To Browsers With Open Source,” Joshi walked through the rapidly maturing ecosystem of JavaScript libraries making high-performance computing accessible directly in the browser.
