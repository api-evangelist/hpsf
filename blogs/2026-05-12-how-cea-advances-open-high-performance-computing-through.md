The French Atomic Energy Commission is a leading European research institution with deep expertise in High Performance Computing. As an operator of one of Europe’s first upcoming exascale supercomputers, CEA plays a central role in shaping the future of large scale scientific computing. Its involvement in the High Performance Software Foundation (HPSF) reflects a clear commitment to strengthening open source software for the global HPC community.
